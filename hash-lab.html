Hash Lab

A hands-on tool for understanding what makes passwords weak — and why hashing, salting, and slow algorithms matter. Everything runs locally in your browser.

This lab works two ways: check a password's real-world strength, or attempt to recover a password from a hash you already have (an old export, a CTF challenge, your own records). It does not attempt logins, WiFi networks, or any live system.

    Generated locally using the browser's cryptographically secure random number generator (crypto.getRandomValues) — nothing is sent anywhere, and nothing is stored after you leave the page.

    Built for learning: entropy math, dictionary/mutation attacks, and why slow hashing (bcrypt/scrypt/argon2) beats fast hashing (MD5/SHA) for storing real passwords. No network requests are made except the optional breach check, which you trigger manually.
